# Beta waitlist

People join the UnifiedDay TestFlight beta waitlist at
https://deiondrickroberts.com/unifiedday-beta.

## How it works

1. Someone submits the Framer form on the beta page.
2. The form sends the signup to Make through a webhook.
3. Make adds a new row to the Beta Testers database in Notion.
4. TestFlight invites go to people on the waitlist first.

The waitlist runs outside this app and repo. The app has no part in it.

Keep webhook URLs, API keys, and database IDs out of this repo.
